Shram Bhoj is a hyper-local, real-time marketplace connecting India's 450 million manual workers—plumbers, electricians, carpenters, masons, and factory laborers—with employers seeking their skills. Unlike traditional job platforms designed for white-collar professionals, Shram Bhoj specifically addresses the unique challenges faced by blue-collar workers.

The platform eliminates middlemen and contractors who typically capture 30% of workers' wages, ensuring fair compensation through direct employer-worker connections. Workers create verified digital profiles featuring their trade, experience, skill level, and Aadhaar-based verification badges, establishing a credible online presence previously unavailable to them.

Employers post jobs with specific requirements and instantly receive matched worker profiles based on trade, location, and availability. The system supports urgent hiring needs while maintaining quality through worker ratings and job completion history.

Built with React and TypeScript, the mobile-first design ensures accessibility for workers primarily using smartphones. Supabase provides secure, real-time database operations with Row Level Security protecting user data. The platform delivers essential marketplace features including worker discovery, job posting, application management, and comprehensive dashboards.

Shram Bhoj empowers India's workforce with digital identity, fair wage access, and the infrastructure needed to participate in the country's $455 billion gig economy, transforming informal labor markets into transparent, efficient ecosystems.
